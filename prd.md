# Product Requirements Document (PRD): Anas Watches Store

## 1. Product Overview
**Anas Watches Store** is an elegant, modern, and high-end e-commerce platform dedicated to selling used luxury watches in Pakistan. The platform aims to provide a trustworthy and premium shopping experience with a highly visual, animated, and responsive design.

## 2. Target Audience
- Individuals looking for used luxury watches in Pakistan.
- Professionals seeking premium timepieces.
- Students and the younger generation interested in high-end fashion.

## 3. Design & User Experience (UX)
- **Theme:** Luxury, minimal, and high-end with a striking **black and gold contrast**.
- **Inspiration:** "Nordwatch Luxury UI" (Figma).
- **Interactivity:** Smooth, modern animations and interactive product sliders.
- **Standards:** Fully responsive (mobile-friendly), accessible, clean code, and SEO optimized.

### 3.1 Home Page & Hero Section
- **Hero Section (Landing):** Designed to be the absolute best and most professional hero section. It will feature a high-end visual of a luxury watch (e.g., `200.jpg`), a strong headline, and a "Shop Now" CTA. It will utilize top-tier, ultra-smooth GSAP animations on page load for a stunning, premium first impression.
- **Product Display:** A showcase of around 30 to 32 watches, featuring real luxury designs (like Hublot, etc.).
- **Pricing:** Prices will range from **4,000 PKR to 12,000 PKR**.
- **Product Cards:** Every product will display a high-quality image, the product name, price, and a **customer rating**.

## 4. Core Features

### 4.1 Customer Features
- **Product Catalog & Details:** Browse watches with high-quality images and descriptions.
- **Shopping Cart:** Add/remove items and proceed to checkout easily.
- **Wishlist:** Save favorite watches for later.
- **User Profiles:** Manage personal information, addresses, and secure data.
- **Order Management:** View order history and track active orders (Pending, Shipped, Delivered).
